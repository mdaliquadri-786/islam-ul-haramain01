All published educational articles pass through a strict multi-stage peer-review state machine:
```text
DRAFT  ──>  SUBMITTED_FOR_REVIEW  ──>  UNDER_REVIEW  ──>  APPROVED  ──>  PUBLISHED
  │                                        │
  └── (Author cannot self-approve)         └── (Changes Requested / Rejected)
```
1. **Self-Approval Prohibition:** `trg_prevent_author_self_approval` prevents any author from approving their own work.
2. **Independent Scholar Assignment:** A designated scholar reviewer evaluates textual citations, theological coherence, and attribution.
3. **Immutable Revision History:** Once approved, revisions are locked against edits. Any modification creates a new revision `N+1` requiring fresh scholar approval.
