# Aqeedah Governance & Theological Taxonomy
**Platform:** ISLAM UL HARAMAIN (إسلام الحرمين)  
**Document Type:** Theological Architecture & Classification Governance  
**Document Version:** 2.0.0 (Revised Specification)  
**Authority:** Approved by Project Owner  
**Date:** September 2026 / Rabi' al-Awwal 1448 AH  

---

## 1. Tripartite Governance Framework

All theological documentation, classification, and database modeling on ISLAM UL HARAMAIN must strictly differentiate:
* **Category A (Owner Approved):** Methodological positions formally selected by the project owner.
* **Category B (Requires Scholarly Verification):** Classical claims, source citations, historical narrations, and theological nuances that must be verified against primary texts before publication.
* **Category C (Architectural Governance Rules):** System and schema rules enforcing source attribution, controversy flags, immutability, and review gates.

---

## 2. Theological Foundations & Multi-Tradition Recognition

### 2.1 Multi-Tradition Scope [Category A: Owner Approved]
The platform recognizes that classical Sunni theological articulation developed primarily across three recognized traditions:
1. **The Athari Tradition (المدرسة الأثرية / أهل الحديث)**
2. **The Ash'ari Tradition (المدرسة الأشعرية)**
3. **The Maturidi Tradition (المدرسة الماتريدية)**

### 2.2 Anti-Sectarian Architectural Rules [Category C: Architectural Rule]
* The platform must not enforce the specialized dialectical terminology of one Sunni theological school as an exclusive, universal truth predicate to the delegitimization of the others.
* **Prohibition of Sectarian Takfir:** No content on the platform may declare an established Sunni theological tradition as outside the fold of Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama'ah merely due to differences in technical terminology, *Ta'weel Tanzīhī*, or *Tafweed*.

## 3. Specific Theological Formulations & Guardrails

### 3.1 Tawassul (التوسل)
* **Adopted Methodological Position [Category A: Owner Approved]:**
  Tawassul through Allah's muqarrab/righteous servants (*Awliya*) and through recognized religious means is established and permissible according to the adopted methodology and must be separately documented.
* **Differentiating Categories [Category C: Architectural Rule]:**
  The architecture must differentiate various forms of Tawassul (e.g., through Allah's Names and Attributes, through righteous deeds, through living pious persons, through the person or rank of the Prophet ﷺ and Awliya, and contested phrasings).
* **Source Verification Mandate [Category B: Requires Verification]:**
  * The platform must **NOT** automatically claim that any specific form of Tawassul represents the "majority Sunni position across all four madhhabs" unless established through verified, cited classical sources.
  * Every documented position must record:
    * Exact scholar and school
    * Original book, volume, page, and edition
    * Specific textual evidence (*daleel*) cited
    * Terminology used by the author
    * Documented scholarly disagreement where applicable

### 3.2 Awliya, Istighatha & Independent Divine Power (القدرة الذاتية المستقلة)
* **Foundational Creed [Category A: Owner Approved]:**
  * **Allah alone possesses independent divine power (*al-qudrah al-dhatiyyah al-mustaqillah*).**
  * Awliya Allah do not possess independent / zati divine power.
  * Allah may grant His servants abilities, means, miracles (*Karamat*), assistance, or other effects strictly by His power and permission (*bi-idhn Allah wa qadratih*).
* **Expressions of Address [Category A: Owner Approved]:**
  * Expressions such as *"Ya Rasul Allah"* (يا رسول الله), *"Ya Ali"* (يا علي), *"Ya Ghawth"* (يا غوث), or *"Ya Shaykh"* (يا شيخ) are not to be automatically condemned merely because of their wording.
  * The project owner's approved position is that these expressions are permitted within the adopted theological framework.
* **Scholarly & Linguistic Nuance [Category B: Requires Verification & Category C: Rule]:**
  * The platform must **NOT impose "Nida' Majazi" (metaphorical address) as a universal condition** unless a particular cited scholar or source explicitly establishes that condition.
  * The platform must **NOT claim that all permissibility is based on metaphorical address**.
  * Future scholarly content must investigate the actual meanings, theological assumptions, linguistic context, and scholarly positions surrounding such expressions, supported by verified literature.

### 3.3 Mawlid al-Nabi ﷺ (المولد النبوي الشريف)
* **Adopted Methodological Position [Category A: Owner Approved]:**
  Mawlid is permissible and is a good deed, provided it is free from unlawful activities.
* **Correction of Classification [Category B: Requires Verification & Category C: Rule]:**
  * The platform must **NOT** independently convert this position into a specific formal legal classification such as *"Mustahabb"* unless that exact classification is supported by the selected scholarly sources.
  * Future content may document:
    * Permissibility
    * Virtue / good deed status
    * Conditions and etiquette
    * Scholarly evidence
    * Opposing scholarly arguments and cautions
  * All documentation must feature exact attribution without manufacturing artificial consensus (*Ijma'*).

### 3.4 Classification of Bid'ah (Religious Innovation - البدعة)
* **Adopted Framework [Category A: Owner Approved]:**
  The platform recognizes the classical two-fold classification:
  1. **Bid'ah Hasanah / Mahmudah (بدعة حسنة):** Newly originated matters conforming to the general principles, objectives, and spirit of the Shari'ah.
  2. **Bid'ah Sayyi'ah / Dalalah (بدعة سيئة):** Newly originated matters contradicting, altering, or opposing the established Qur'an, Sunnah, and consensus.
* **Data Model & Terminology Governance [Category C: Architectural Rule]:**
  * The database schema must **never** reduce Bid'ah to a simple boolean field (`bidah: true/false`).
  * The architecture must support detailed classification, legal rationale, and scholar attribution.
  * Where scholars use different terminology or classification systems, preserve their specific attribution and analysis.

### 3.5 Tabarruk (Seeking Blessings - التبرك)
* **Adopted Position [Category A: Owner Approved]:**
  Tabarruk through sacred and virtuous means is permissible and is not inherently haram.
* **Relic Authenticity Verification Rule [Category B: Requires Verification & Category C: Rule]:**
  * Claims regarding the authenticity of specific historical relics must **NOT** be asserted as factual methodology claims.
  * **Future Verification Rule:** Every claim regarding the authenticity or provenance of a physical relic must be independently sourced, historically investigated, and reviewed before publication.
* **Structured Documentation [Category C: Architectural Rule]:**
  The platform will document classical discussions regarding Tabarruk through the Prophet's ﷺ relics, righteous individuals, sacred times, and sacred places, while clearly delineating prohibited practices (such as worshiping objects or attributing independent power to relics).

### 3.6 Shafa'ah (Intercession - الشفاعة)
* **Approved Distinction Requirements [Category A: Owner Approved & Category C: Rule]:**
  The architecture must maintain a detailed distinction between:
  1. Shafa'ah established in the Qur'an and Sunnah.
  2. Shafa'ah on the Day of Judgment.
  3. Allah alone granting and permitting Shafa'ah (*Lillahi al-shafa'atu jami'a*).
  4. Seeking and interpreting Shafa'ah.
  5. Disputed forms and formulations.
* **Verification Mandate [Category B: Requires Verification]:** All substantive conclusions must later be sourced directly from verified theological texts.

### 3.7 Ziyarat al-Qubur (Visiting Graves - زيارة القبور)
* **Detailed Multi-Category Framework [Category A: Owner Approved & Category C: Rule]:**
  The platform will not collapse grave visitation into a single binary ruling. The architecture must support distinct treatments for:
  1. Visiting graves generally (remembrance of the Hereafter, greeting the deceased).
  2. Visiting the Rawdah and grave of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in Madinah.
  3. Grave etiquette and Sunnah supplications.
  4. Supplication (*Dua*) for the deceased.
  5. Seeking Allah's grace near the graves of the righteous.
  6. Disputed practices and historical differences among jurists.
  7. Prohibited practices and innovated excesses.
  8. Directing worship (*'Ibadah*), vows, sacrifices, or prostration of worship to other than Allah (strictly prohibited).

### 3.8 Ahl al-Bayt, Sahabah & Sayyiduna Mu'awiyah (رضي الله عنهم)
* **Central Historical Categories [Category A: Owner Approved]:**
  * Ahl al-Bayt, Khulafa al-Rashidun, and the Sahabah (رضي الله عنهم) are central historical and religious categories.
  * Careless slander or disparagement is prohibited.
* **Sayyiduna Mu'awiyah ibn Abi Sufyan (رضي الله عنه) [Category A: Owner Approved & Category B/C]:**
  * The project owner mandates that the platform must **investigate historical reports rather than blindly suppressing them or blindly repeating them**.
  * The system must distinguish:
    * Established historical reports (*Thabit*)
    * Weak historical reports (*Da'if*)
    * Disputed or polemical reports
    * Later historical interpretations
    * Classical historical critique
    * Sunni scholarly treatment
  * **Architectural Rule:** Do not automatically convert either praise or accusation into a database fact. Do not suppress historically documented material merely because it is uncomfortable. Do not manufacture accusations.

### 3.9 Yazid ibn Mu'awiyah
* **Owner Methodological Preference / View [Category A: Owner Preference]:**
  * The project owner personally holds the position that Yazid was an illegitimate tyrant and kafir.
* **INVIOLABLE ARCHITECTURAL RULE [Category C: Architectural Rule]:**
  > **The platform MUST NOT encode `Yazid = kafir` as an objective universal database fact.**
* **Documenting Scholarly Spectrum [Category B: Requires Verification & Category C: Rule]:**
  * Future content must document actual Sunni scholarly positions with exact source attribution rather than assuming a fixed, unverified number of positions.
  * At minimum, the architecture must support distinguishing:
    1. Takfir
    2. Fisq / condemnation without takfir
    3. Cursing (*La'nah*)
    4. Restraint / silence (*Tawwaquf*)
    5. Other documented scholarly positions
  * Only include a position when a reliable, verified source supports it. Mandatory multi-scholar review required.

## 4. Sunni / Shia Comparative Boundaries

### 4.1 Comparative Scope [Category A: Owner Approved]
The comparative framework must address:
1. Status and treatment of the Sahabah.
2. Religious exaggeration (*Mubalaghah* / *Ghuluw*).
3. The doctrine of the Imamate (divine appointment, infallibility, cosmic authority).
4. **The Twelve Imams of Ahl al-Bayt [Category A: Owner Approved]:** The project owner's approved methodological understanding is that the Twelve Imams of Ahl al-Bayt are recognized as Imams of their respective times and were Imams for the people, while the platform must distinguish this Sunni understanding from the specific doctrinal claims made by Twelver Shi'ism regarding Imamate, infallibility, and authority.
5. Sources of religious authority.
6. Foundational doctrinal differences.

### 4.2 Objective Comparative Standards [Category B: Requires Verification & Category C: Rule]
* **Scholarly & Historical Verification [Category B: Requires Verification]:**
  * Do not invent historical evidence or manufacture artificial Sunni consensus.
  * Do not automatically equate the Sunni concept of Imam with the Twelver theological doctrine of Imamate.
  * The exact historical and theological details, narrations, and scholarly evaluations must be documented later with reliable Sunni sources and, where appropriate, primary Twelver sources.
* Do **NOT** independently insert sweeping theological conclusions unless part of a properly sourced comparative study.
* Content must clearly distinguish:
  * **SUNNI POSITION** (with primary Sunni citations)
  * **TWELVER SHIA POSITION** (with primary Shia citations)
* Content must remain free from abusive or insulting language.
